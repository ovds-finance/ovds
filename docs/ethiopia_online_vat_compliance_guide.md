# Ethiopia Online VAT Declaration Workflow & Compliance Guide

This comprehensive guide details the electronic Value Added Tax (VAT) filing workflow using the **Ethiopian Ministry of Revenues (MoR) eFiling system**, updated with regulations applicable in 2026. It outlines prerequisites, step-by-step filing procedures, payment methods, penalty structures, and the process for submitting amended declarations.

---

### 📋 1. Prerequisites & System Access

Before beginning the online VAT declaration, taxpayers must ensure they possess the necessary credentials and documentation:

*   **Active eFiling Account:** Regulated businesses must be registered on the official [Ethiopian Ministry of Revenues eFiling Portal](https://www.mor.gov.et/).
*   **Valid TIN:** A 10-digit Taxpayer Identification Number.
*   **Digital Certificates:** Secure authentication keys or credentials provided by the MoR during physical enrollment.
*   **Filing Frequency:** VAT declarations must be submitted monthly, no later than the **last day of the month following the tax period** (e.g., January's VAT return is due by February 28/29).
*   **Source Documents:** Commercially verifiable Sales Invoices (Output VAT) and Purchase Invoices/Customs Declarations (Input VAT).

---

### ⚙️ 2. Step-by-Step eFiling Workflow

The online declaration translates physical financial records into the electronic portal via the following structured sequence:

#### Step 2.1: Authentication & Tax Period Selection
1. Access the eFiling portal and log in using your authorized company credentials.
2. Navigate to the **Declarations** module and select **Value Added Tax (VAT)**.
3. Choose the specific tax year and month (tax period) you are declaring.

#### Step 2.2: Declaring Output VAT (Sales Data)
1. **Standard Rated Sales:** Enter the total gross value of local sales subject to the standard 15% VAT rate. The system automatically computes the corresponding 15% tax.
2. **Zero-Rated Sales:** Input the gross value of qualifying exports or zero-rated domestic supplies (0% VAT).
3. **Exempt Sales:** Declare transactions that are legally exempt from VAT. These do not attract tax, and associated input VAT cannot be claimed.

#### Step 2.3: Declaring Input VAT (Purchase Data)
1. **Local Purchases:** Input the taxable value and VAT paid on raw materials, stock, or allowable capital assets purchased from VAT-registered domestic suppliers.
2. **Imported Goods:** Enter details from customs clearance documents (Asycuda records), specifying the VAT paid at the port of entry.
3. **Withholding VAT:** If applicable, account for VAT withheld by authorized government agencies or designated large share companies acting as withholding agents.

#### Step 2.4: System Reconciliation & Validation
*   The eFiling system cross-references your input VAT claims with the corresponding sellers' output VAT submissions where integrated.
*   **Automated Calculation:** The system performs the net calculation:
    $$\text{Net VAT Payable/Credit} = \text{Total Output VAT} - \text{Total Input VAT}$$
*   If Output exceeds Input, a **VAT Liability** is generated.
*   If Input exceeds Output, a **VAT Credit** is carried forward, or a refund claim can be initiated for qualifying entities (such as continuous exporters).

---

### 💳 3. Digital Payment Fulfillment

Once the declaration is verified and submitted, the system generates an electronic **Payment Order Number (PRN)**. Payments can be settled through the following unified digital channels:

1. **Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE):** Payment via CBE Birr, retail internet banking, or over-the-counter using the system-generated PRN.
2. **Telebirr SuperApp:** Integration with the **Telebirr Remit/Gov Payment** ecosystem for instant corporate wallet settlements.
3. **Other Commercial Banks:** Utilizing the national payment switch (EthSwitch) to transfer funds directly to the Ministry of Revenues' treasury accounts using the PRN.

---

### ⚠️ 4. Late Filing Penalties & Interest Rates

Failing to declare or pay VAT within the statutory timeframe triggers harsh statutory financial penalties under Ethiopian tax law:

*   **Late Filing Penalty:** 
    *   A penalty of **1,000 Birr** for the first month (or part thereof) the return is late.
    *   An additional **2,000 Birr** for each subsequent month that the declaration remains unfiled.
*   **Late Payment Penalty:** A penalty equal to **5%** of the unpaid tax amount for the first month, plus an additional **1%** of the unpaid tax for each additional month or part thereof that the tax remains unpaid.
*   **Interest Charges:** Calculated on a monthly basis, interest is pegged to the highest commercial lending rate prevailing in Ethiopia plus a statutory premium (typically around **1.25% to 1.5% per month** compounded), applied from the original due date until the actual payment date.

### 🔄 5. Amended & Corrected Declarations

If an error or omission is discovered after submitting a VAT return, taxpayers must file an **Amended Declaration** via the portal:

1. Navigate to **Submitted Declarations** and locate the specific tax period.
2. Select **Request Amendment** (Note: Amendments are restricted if the MoR has already initiated a formal tax audit for that period).
3. Provide a written justification or select a standardized error code specifying why the revision is necessary.
4. Update the incorrect figures (e.g., uploading an omitted input invoice or correcting a typographical transposition error).
5. **Re-submit:** The system computes the differential. 
    *   If you owe more tax, a new PRN is issued, and late payment penalties/interest may apply backdated to the original deadline.
    *   If you overpaid, the balance is subjected to review and added as an approved credit carryforward.
